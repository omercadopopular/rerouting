# Generated replication archive

This local directory contains generated outputs moved out of `data/analysis/passthru_data` and `data/verification/passthru_data` after the historical methodology was locked. The files include exploratory bridge runs, policy versions, extension versions, checkpoints, and figures.

The generated contents are ignored by Git because they are large and reproducible. The versioned `archive_manifest.json` records their scope. Canonical active outputs are under `data/processed`, and publication figures are under `figs/replication`.

Raw Census archives, official tariff-source files, the Fajgelbaum replication package, CPI inputs, and manuscript sources were not moved here.
